# SiteLens: Strategic SEO Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
SiteLens is a unified search visibility and site intelligence platform designed to integrate SEO, GEO, MEO, and AIO metrics into a single, actionable dashboard. This strategy outlines the approach to building organic search authority for the SiteLens SaaS platform from zero, leveraging its unique positioning of "Analysis → Visualization → Improvement Proposal → Continuous Monitoring."

## 2. Target Audience
- **Web Production Companies / Agencies**: Seeking reporting efficiency and integrated dashboards for their clients.
- **SMEs (Small and Medium Enterprises)**: Looking for an easy-to-understand, all-in-one visibility tool without complex configurations.
- **SEO Operators / Marketers**: Needing cross-channel visibility tracking (traditional search + AI search).
- **Store Operators**: Requiring integrated local (MEO) and general search visibility.

## 3. Goals & KPIs (6-Month Targets)
- **Organic Traffic**: 3,000 sessions / month
- **Free Trial Registrations**: 50 / month
- **Document Requests / Demo Bookings**: 20 / month
- **Branded Searches (指名検索)**: 500+ / month
- **Keyword Rankings**: Top 10 for 10 primary commercial keywords (e.g., "SEO 分析ツール 統合", "GEO 対策 ツール", "MEO ダッシュボード").

## 4. Core SEO Strategy
### 4.1. Differentiation via Next-Gen Search (GEO/AIO)
Traditional SEO tools dominate standard keywords. SiteLens will capture early authority in emerging categories: **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** and **AIO (AI Optimization)**, alongside MEO and SEO integration. 

### 4.2. "Jobs-to-be-Done" Content Approach
Target high-intent, bottom-of-funnel (BOFU) queries such as "複数サイト SEO 管理ツール" (Multi-site SEO management tool) or "Search Console PageSpeed Insights 連携" (GSC PSI integration). 

### 4.3. Resource Allocation
- **Budget**: 10万〜30万円 / month (Outsourcing used strictly for writing assistance).
- **Velocity**: 4-8 articles / month.
- **Production**: In-house focus on LP optimization, technical SEO, and expert content reviews to ensure high E-E-A-T.
