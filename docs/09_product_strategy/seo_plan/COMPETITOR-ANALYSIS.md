# SiteLens: Competitor Analysis

## 1. Competitor Landscape

### Tier 1: Direct/Traditional Giants
**Ahrefs, Semrush, Screaming Frog, Sitebulb**
- **Strengths**: Massive proprietary data (backlinks, global keyword volumes), deep technical crawling capabilities.
- **Weaknesses**: Steep learning curve, expensive for SMEs, overwhelming UI/UX for non-experts, lack of focus on emerging AI search (GEO) and local/MEO integration.
- **SiteLens Opportunity**: Offer a cleaner, integrated view focused on *actionability* rather than raw data dumps. Highlight the "Analysis → Visualization → Improvement → Monitoring" workflow.

### Tier 2: Niche Optimization Tools
**Surfer, Clearscope (Content/On-page) / BrightLocal, Moz Local (MEO)**
- **Strengths**: Highly specialized in their specific verticals.
- **Weaknesses**: Fragmented toolstack. A user needs multiple subscriptions.
- **SiteLens Opportunity**: Position SiteLens as the unified dashboard that brings local (MEO) and general (SEO/GEO) tracking into a single pane of glass.

### Indirect Competitors: Free Native Tools
**PageSpeed Insights, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Lighthouse, Google Business Profile**
- **Strengths**: Free, authoritative, source-of-truth data.
- **Weaknesses**: Siloed data. Requires manual compilation to see the "big picture" or to generate client reports. No native cross-metric alerting.
- **SiteLens Opportunity**: SiteLens is not competing with them; it is *enhancing* them. Emphasize "API aggregation and automation."

## 2. Keyword & Content Gap Analysis
- **Gap**: There is little authoritative content bridging traditional SEO with GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AIO. 
- **Gap**: "How to combine GSC, GA4, and PSI data effectively for client reporting."
- **Action**: Create comparison pages (`sitelens vs [competitor]`) that highlight SiteLens's integrated reporting capabilities vs. the siloed nature of competitors.
