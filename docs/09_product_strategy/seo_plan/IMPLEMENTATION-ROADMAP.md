# SiteLens: SEO Implementation Roadmap

## Overview
A 12-month phased roadmap to achieve 3,000 monthly organic sessions, 50 trials/month, and top 10 rankings for 10 primary commercial keywords.

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 0-2)
**Focus: Strategy Design & Technical Infrastructure**
- [ ] **Technical SEO Setup**: Configure Next.js App Router metadata, dynamic XML sitemaps, and `robots.txt`.
- [ ] **Schema Markup**: Implement `SoftwareApplication`, `Organization`, `WebSite`, and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD schemas.
- [ ] **Core Pages Launch**: Deploy Home, Features, Pricing, and Use Case LPs.
- [ ] **Analytics**: Integrate GA4, PostHog, and GSC. Set up conversion tracking for Trials and Document Requests.
- [ ] **Performance**: Ensure the SiteLens marketing site achieves a Lighthouse score of 90+ across all metrics (Eating our own dog food).

### Phase 2: Expansion (Months 3-4)
**Focus: Content Addition & Comparison Pages**
- [ ] **Content Engine**: Ramp up article production (4-8/month) using external writers + in-house technical editing.
- [ ] **Comparison LPs**: Launch "SiteLens vs X" pages targeting users overwhelmed by complex traditional tools.
- [ ] **E-E-A-T Signals**: Add author bios, product screenshots, and technical transparency to build trust.
- [ ] **Monitoring**: Begin tracking keyword rankings for early content in GSC.

### Phase 3: CV Optimization (Months 5-6)
**Focus: Conversion Improvement & Internal Linking**
- [ ] **CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization)**: A/B test CTAs on high-traffic blog posts and LPs.
- [ ] **Lead Magnets**: Introduce downloadable whitepapers (e.g., "The 2026 Guide to GEO & AIO") to drive the 20/mo Document Request KPI.
- [ ] **Internal Linking**: Audit and optimize the pillar-cluster structure. Ensure no orphaned pages.
- [ ] **6-Month Evaluation**: Check progress against the 3,000 organic traffic / 50 trials KPIs. Adjust content velocity as needed.

### Phase 4: Scale & Authority (Months 7-12)
**Focus: Primary Acquisition Channel Maturation**
- [ ] **Backlink Strategy**: Digital PR, guest posting, and unique data studies using SiteLens's own analytics capabilities.
- [ ] **Case Studies**: Publish user success stories to boost BOFU conversions.
- [ ] **Advanced Content**: Deep dives into programmatic SEO and GEO algorithm updates.
- [ ] **Goal**: Establish SEO as the undisputed primary customer acquisition channel for SiteLens.
